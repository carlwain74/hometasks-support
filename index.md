# Home Task - Manager: Support

Home Task - Manager is an app for tracking household tasks, with categories, due dates, notes, and iCloud sync between your own devices.

## Need help?

Email [carlwainwright74@gmail.com](mailto:carlwainwright74@gmail.com) and include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

## Common questions

**My tasks aren't syncing.** Check that you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on each device, that iCloud is turned on for the app, and that you have internet access. The iCloud Sync row in the app's Settings shows the current status.

**How do I delete all my tasks?** Open Settings in the app and choose "Clear all tasks". This removes them from all of your devices.

**Is my data private?** Yes. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
